# Album 119 — A New and Living Way
The Veil Torn
 
The veil, the Ark, the mercy seat, the Day of Atonement, and the access opened by the blood of Jesus.
 
# Track List:
1. The Curtain of Blue, Purple, and Scarlet (Teaching – Exodus 26)
2. Cherubim Woven and Golden (Poetic)
3. The Ark and the Mercy Seat (Narrative)
4. Once a Year with Blood (Teaching – Leviticus 16)
5. Torn from Top to Bottom (Spoken Story – Matthew 27:51)
6. Through His Flesh (Teaching – Hebrews 10:19–20)
7. Confidence to Enter (Promising Song)
8. The True Mercy Seat (Praise – Romans 3:25)
9. Draw Near with a True Heart (Praising)
* (these are songs drawn from Scripture’s own sanctuary and creation accounts)
    
# Study note: 
The veil guarded the way; Christ’s body is the veil torn so that the way stands open.

# Prayer note: 
Lord Jesus, by Your blood we enter. Keep us near.
